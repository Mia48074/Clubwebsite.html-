Oakland Tech Jewelry for Joy Club

Welcome to Jewelry for Joy! This is a hands-on creative community where students design, craft, and share handmade accessories. All tools and starter materials are provided, and no prior crafting experience is needed.

Why Join Us?

  • Learn practical design skills
  • Create thoughtful pieces that are donated to those in need
  • Unwind and connect
